How this works. Every change you make below is saved instantly to this browser, so you can open the Projects page right away and see it — no download needed just to preview. That preview is only visible on this device, though. To make changes visible to actual visitors:
  1. Click "Download projects-data.js" once you're happy with everything.
  2. Replace the file at assets/data/projects-data.js in your site folder with the one you downloaded.
  3. Re-upload the site to your host.
  4. Come back here and click "Mark as published" so this browser stops showing you the old draft preview.
