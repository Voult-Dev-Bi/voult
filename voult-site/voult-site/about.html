Who we are

About VOULT

A small, specialist BIM practice based in Calicut, India, built around the belief that most construction rework starts as a coordination problem, not a site problem.

Our story

We started VOULT because the expensive mistakes were always the avoidable ones.

Duct runs fighting beams. Structure clashing with risers. Design intent that never quite matched what got issued for construction. Every one of those is cheap to fix in a model and expensive to fix on site. VOULT exists to catch them in the model, as a focused BIM coordination and design consulting practice working across complex, multi-discipline projects, including the team behind King Salman Park in Saudi Arabia.

What we hold to
01

Model first

If it isn't resolved in the model, it isn't resolved. We don't let "we'll sort it on site" become the plan.

02

Stay small, stay sharp

We take on work we can coordinate properly, not everything we're offered. A small team means nothing gets lost between people.

03

Build for handover

A model that dies at practical completion wasn't worth building. Ours are built to keep working for facilities teams.
